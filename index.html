The Building

A Santa Cruz landmark, reimagined for business.

Branciforte Plaza opened in 1929 as the Santa Cruz Hospital, designed in the Italian Renaissance style with classical details. In 1951 it became the Santa Cruz Hospital of the Adrian Dominican Sisters, and in the 1970s it was adapted into the office plaza it is today.

Tenants enjoy a grand arched entrance, a central fountain courtyard, landscaped grounds and a large private parking lot, a short walk from Downtown, Seabright and the San Lorenzo River.
